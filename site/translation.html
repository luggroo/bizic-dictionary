土家语可以翻译。

汉语可以译成土家语，土家语也可以译成汉语。北部土家语、南部土家语都可以翻译。

词典、语法和语料为土家语翻译提供依据。人工翻译和大语言模型翻译都可以查阅这些资料，按各自方言组织表达。

Tujia can be translated.

Chinese can be translated into Tujia, and Tujia into Chinese. Both Northern and Southern Tujia can be translated.

Dictionaries, grammars and recorded texts provide a basis for Tujia translation. Human translators and large language models can consult these resources and use the vocabulary and grammar of the chosen variety.
